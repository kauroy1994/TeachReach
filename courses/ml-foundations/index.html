09Syllabus preview

ASSESSMENT DESIGN

Practice first. Demonstrate understanding in class.

  1. Study each module's slides, visual lab, notebook, and worked examples.
  2. Complete practice exercises with feedback; repeat them as often as needed.
  3. At the end of the module, complete a new in-class variant covering equivalent learning outcomes. The instructor grades this assessment.

There is a graded in-class variant at the end of each of the 16 modules, including the capstone module.

Assessment and grading →
WHAT STUDENTS WILL ACHIEVE

Explain, derive, implement, and evaluate ML.

  • Represent data and derive mathematical learning objectives.
  • Train and compare supervised, unsupervised, and neural models.
  • Understand attention, LLMs, generative models, and reinforcement learning from first principles.
  • Evaluate generalization, calibration, robustness, and limitations.
  • Document and defend an integrated end-to-end ML study.
