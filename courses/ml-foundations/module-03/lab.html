1. Which prediction fits better?

Ava scored 60. Start at 55, then move to 56. Read right to find a higher predicted mean and up to find a larger loss. Which prediction gives the smaller loss? The short line shows which way loss changes near the selected point.

2. Measure the slope at 55

Start at 55 every time. Choose a trial move h. Read how far the loss falls and divide by the distance you moved. Predict which number the ratio approaches as you shrink the move.

Try it. Why does the ratio approach −0.05 even though the loss itself stays positive here?

Reveal answerHide answer

The ratio measures a change. Moving a little to the right of 55 lowers the loss. A decrease has a negative sign.

3. Choose one learning rate

Each trial starts with the original prediction of 55 and gradients [−0.10, −0.15, −0.05]. Try rates 1, 10, and 16. Before each change, predict whether the loss will fall.
