WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Module learning outcomes

  • Distinguish population objectives from empirical objectives.
  • Describe sampling variation and assumptions of a hypothesis class.
  • Recognize data leakage, inductive bias, and poor generalization.
LEARNING SEQUENCE

How this lesson builds

  1. Set aside a test group before choosing a rule. Call the students used for fitting the training sample. A population is the larger group you want the rule to work for.
  2. Compute errors of 2 and 4 on two training students. Their average, 3, is the observed training risk for absolute error. Expected risk is the average we would obtain across the population.
  3. Try a constant rule and a straight line. The set of rules we allow is a hypothesis class. Choosing straight lines expresses a preference for simple relationships, an inductive bias.
  4. Compare the fixed rule on new students. Show how choosing it after inspecting their answers leaks test information into training.
