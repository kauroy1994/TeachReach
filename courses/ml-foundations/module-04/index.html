WHAT YOU'LL LEARN

Module learning outcomes

  • Distinguish population objectives from empirical objectives.
  • Describe sampling variation and assumptions of a hypothesis class.
  • Recognize data leakage, inductive bias, and poor generalization.
LEARNING SEQUENCE

One consistent teaching approach

  1. Begin with a concrete example and a recap of the mathematics from earlier modules.
  2. Examine visual intuition, then derive the corresponding equations and assumptions.
  3. Implement the core operations with PyTorch and compare model behavior.
  4. Complete practice exercises and prepare for an end-of-module in-class variant.
