Where did least squares come from?

Start with a prediction error measured in score points. Squaring gives a smooth penalty and prevents positive and negative misses from canceling. Module 02 adds a probabilistic reason: under independent Gaussian errors with common variance, maximizing the likelihood selects the line with the smallest sum of squares.

Does logistic regression predict a number or a class?

It first predicts a probability between zero and one. A separate threshold converts that probability into a class when a decision is needed. Raising the threshold can change the class while leaving the fitted model untouched.

Why not treat a predicted probability as a guarantee?

A probability summarizes a model’s uncertainty about a category. A forecast of 0.8 still assigns probability 0.2 to the opposite outcome. Repeated held-out cases reveal whether such probabilities are useful in the population of interest.
