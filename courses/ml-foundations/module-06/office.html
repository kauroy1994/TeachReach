How can kNN learn if there are no weights to fit?

It retains labeled training examples and a distance rule. At prediction time their locations and labels determine the votes. The examples themselves affect every new decision, while choices such as scaling and k determine what neighborhood means.

What does a support vector contribute?

In the clean one-dimensional example, the negative point at 1 and the positive point at 3 constrain how far a separating threshold can sit from either class. Moving either can move the midpoint. Removing a distant point at 0 leaves these two constraints intact.

Should an ensemble always be more accurate?

No. Its members may repeat the same mistake or outvote the most accurate member. On the lesson’s three held-out examples, the neighbor alone gets all three right and the ensemble gets two. Examine agreement and errors on independent data.

How do I compare a tree and a neighbor model fairly?

Give them the same training/validation/test split. Select the depth or neighbor count with validation cases, fit preprocessing using training data and evaluate the chosen procedure with the same held-out targets and metric.
