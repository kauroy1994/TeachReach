How can a method be supervised by something other than a person?

A recorded value or a known relationship supplies the answer to a deliberately constructed task. We can hide one measured value or record that two crops came from the same source. A person still chooses what counts as a meaningful task or transformation.

Is a low reconstruction error enough to choose an embedding?

Consider a decoder with access to the whole unmasked input. It could copy the answer and ignore the embedding. Inspect what information enters each path, then test whether the embedding helps on a separate task.

Why freeze the encoder while evaluating the probe?

It lets you test what a simple classifier can already read from the representation. Updating the encoder and classifier together is fine-tuning and measures what the system can learn after receiving downstream labels.

Should an embedding always stay the same when I add noise or change brightness?

That depends on the desired task and the size of the change. A constant offset preserves the signed change of our synthetic trace. It changes the answer to questions about absolute brightness. Test the proposed transformation against the downstream question.
