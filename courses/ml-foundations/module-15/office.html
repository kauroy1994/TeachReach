Can a well-calibrated model still be unhelpful?

Yes. If half the attempts succeed, assigning 0.5 to everyone can match the overall frequency while giving no way to distinguish easier attempts from harder ones. Examine discrimination and the actual decisions as well.

Why do the bin boundaries matter?

A wide bin can average together overprediction in one subgroup and underprediction in another. Very narrow bins may have just a few outcomes. Report the bin definition and count alongside every estimate.

When should the system defer a decision?

A help tool might send uncertain cases for review. Pick the threshold using validation data and the costs of mistakes and review. Report how many cases it covers and how often it errs among both decided and deferred cases.
