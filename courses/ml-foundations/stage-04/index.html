STAGE 4 OUTCOMES

By the end of this stage, you can…

  • Formulate sequential decision problems and solve small reinforcement-learning tasks.
  • Evaluate calibration, robustness, uncertainty, and interpretability.
  • Design and defend a reproducible end-to-end ML investigation.
Stage milestone: Demonstrate reliable judgment about model assumptions, performance, and limitations.
ASSESSMENT THROUGHOUT THE STAGE

Learn, practice, demonstrate.

  1. Read each module's worked explanation and complete its interactive exercises.
  2. Use feedback and the module notebook to understand why the methods work.
  3. At the end of every module, complete a new in-class variant of the exercises administered and graded by the instructor.

Published practice is not the graded variant. Assessment topics and methods are listed on each module page.

MODULE 14 · STAGE 4

Reinforcement Learning and Sequential Decision-Making

Learning to act through rewards

Lessons available

Markov decision processes, states/actions/rewards, policies, value functions, Bellman updates, exploration, and Q-learning.

Learning outcomes

  • Formulate a small sequential decision problem as an MDP.
  • Compute value estimates using Bellman updates.
  • Explain exploration versus exploitation and delayed reward.

Practice & in-class assessment

Module practice covers Gridworld reward and policy explorer; Value-iteration and Q-learning visualization.

The instructor-administered, graded in-class variant at this module's end assesses equivalent skills:

  • Compute a Bellman backup on an unfamiliar state.
  • Choose and explain an action-selection policy.

MODULE 15 · STAGE 4

Trustworthy ML: Uncertainty, Robustness, and Interpretability

Knowing when not to trust a model

Lessons available

Calibration, confidence versus uncertainty, robustness, distribution shift, fairness-aware evaluation, interpretability, and stress testing.

Learning outcomes

  • Diagnose overconfidence and evaluate calibration.
  • Design basic robustness and distribution-shift tests.
  • Interpret explanations while distinguishing association from causation.

Practice & in-class assessment

Module practice covers Reliability diagram and uncertainty lab; Distribution-shift and perturbation explorer.

The instructor-administered, graded in-class variant at this module's end assesses equivalent skills:

  • Find an overconfident model from calibration evidence.
  • Interpret a stress-test failure and recommend a check.

MODULE 16 · STAGE 4

Integrated Machine Learning Capstone

Connecting mathematical understanding to an evaluated ML system

Lessons available

Problem definition, data and target choice, baseline selection, model training, probabilistic interpretation, held-out evaluation, error analysis, and transparent reporting.

Learning outcomes

  • Design a reproducible ML experiment with baselines.
  • Implement and compare models using appropriate metrics.
  • Defend conclusions, assumptions, limitations, and reliability evidence.

Practice & in-class assessment

Module practice covers Model-comparison workbench; End-to-end assessment and error-analysis dashboard.

The instructor-administered, graded in-class variant at this module's end assesses equivalent skills:

  • Defend a model choice and evaluation design.
  • Interpret unseen-data results and propose a justified improvement.
