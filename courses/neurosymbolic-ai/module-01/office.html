Why give a statement an awkward name like Requested(Ava, C1)?

Imagine that Ava writes “Could I borrow C1?” and Bo writes “I’d like to check out C1.” Once a reader confirms that both students are requesting the camera, the desk can use the same relationship to record both requests. The desk can then ask one precise question about the requester and item. The names alone do not decide whether a new note actually expresses a request.

Does False in a Python membership test settle whether something happened?

It settles whether that exact entry occurs in the collection you checked. If the log is incomplete, an event can have happened without an entry. Say “I found no matching entry” until you know the log covers every event relevant to your question.
