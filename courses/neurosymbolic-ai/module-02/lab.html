Explore a case

Counterexample

The complete two-person roster has Oriented(Ava) confirmed and Oriented(Bo) explicitly false.

Does the evidence establish orientation for everyone on the roster, for at least one person, or for both?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The existential claim holds with Ava as its witness. Bo’s explicit no is a counterexample to the universal claim.

Names do not match

Requested(Ava, C1), Eligible(Bo) and Available(C1) are the only relevant records. The ready rule requires the same requester throughout.

Does any request qualify as ready for review?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The only request fixes p as Ava. Eligible(Bo) cannot establish Eligible(Ava), so the rule yields no ready request from these records.

Two eligible requests

Requested(Ava, C1), Requested(Bo, C1), Eligible(Ava), Eligible(Bo) and Available(C1) all hold in the snapshot.

Does the ready rule select one winner?

Reveal the result and reasoning

It derives Ready(Ava, C1) and Ready(Bo, C1). Both requests meet the conditions for review. The rule contains no tie-breaker and makes no allocation.
