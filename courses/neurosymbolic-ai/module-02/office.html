If a borrower is missing from the orientation log, does that disprove the claim that everyone completed orientation?

It depends on what the log records. A complete checked roster with an explicit no supplies a counterexample. A missing entry in a partial log gives an unknown. First identify the set of people in the claim, then check what counts as evidence about each person.

Why keep both Requested and Ready in the model?

Ava can ask for C1 even while it is unavailable. Requested captures her action. Ready captures a conclusion that also depends on eligibility and availability. Keeping both lets the desk explain which condition a pending request still needs.
