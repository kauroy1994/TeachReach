Is the knowledge graph the same thing as the ontology?

The lending graph can record Ava’s request and C1’s location. The ontology supplies terms such as Camera and Equipment and a statement connecting their meanings. A system may store both kinds of statements in the same graph. Together they let you ask both what the desk recorded about this item and what follows from its category.

If I draw an edge between two nodes, does the reverse edge hold too?

Read the edge label in its declared direction. From Ava requested C1 you can look backward to find who requested C1. That operation still answers the original relationship. Claiming that C1 requested Ava would introduce a different statement.
