How does a proof help if someone typed in the wrong fact?

It gives the desk a place to check. If a clerk challenges availability, follow Ready(Ava, C1) to the exact C1 record used by R2. A valid deduction tells us what follows from those premises. Checking the premises against the inventory is a separate task.

Is a list of rule names enough to explain a decision?

R1 and R2 could both be used for many borrowers and cameras. A useful trace identifies Ava, C1, the input records, and the intermediate Eligible(Ava) that connects the two steps. That lets a reviewer replay this particular decision.
