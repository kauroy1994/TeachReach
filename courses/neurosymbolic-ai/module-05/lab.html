Explore a case

At the boundary

The cutoff is 0.75 inclusive. C1 and C2 score 0.750 and 0.749.

For which items should the desk record CapCandidate?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Only CapCandidate(C1). Compare the unrounded scores. C1 meets the cutoff exactly, while C2 falls just below it.

Glare on the lens

The rim cue is 1. The dark-center cue changes from 1 to 0. The weights are 0.6 and 0.3, the bias is −0.1, and the cutoff is 0.75.

How does glare change the proposed fact?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The score falls from 0.8 to 0.5. C1 moves from candidate to inspection. Glare changed the visible evidence even if the physical cap stayed in place.

An independent inspection

C1 scores 0.82. A staff member checks the same photo and reports that the circular object is a reflection.

How should this photo appear in an evaluation?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Record the score, the candidate, and the staff label separately. At a 0.75 cutoff this is a false positive. Retaining both sources makes the error count reproducible.
