Explore a case

Crossed identifiers

Ava reserved C1, Ben reserved C2, and a photo of C2 scores 0.88. The cutoff is 0.75.

Whose review appears, and which item does it name?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Ben's review for C2 appears. The C2 candidate matches his reservation. Ava's C1 still lacks a matching candidate.

One source changes

R17 records Ava and C1. A photo originally scored 0.78. A corrected crop scores 0.71 under the same cutoff of 0.75.

What happens when the adapter and rule run again on the corrected crop?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The corrected crop falls below the cutoff. With no other candidate for C1, Ava's suggestion disappears. R17 stays available as a reservation record.

A correct rule with poor evidence

Ava reserved C1, and the C1 photo scores 0.88. Staff later label the image as cap absent.

Can the matching rule have executed correctly? Where would you investigate?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Yes. It matched C1 and applied the stated cutoff. Inspect the image, features and detector label. The rule trace explains the recommendation even when a premise is unreliable.
