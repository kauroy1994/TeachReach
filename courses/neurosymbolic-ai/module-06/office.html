Which part of this pipeline learned anything?

The detector is the component whose weights can be fitted from examples. The desk supplies the cutoff, the meaning of CapCandidate, and the review rule. A review can change when either the detector or the rule changes. The trace helps distinguish them.

If the system shows a proof trace, can I accept the conclusion?

Read the conclusion carefully. The trace shows why the inputs supported a review suggestion. It also gives you the records to inspect if the photograph was mislabeled or the reservation is stale. The premises determine how much confidence the conclusion deserves.
