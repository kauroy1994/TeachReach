Explore a case

A second camera is nearly as useful

Ava scores C1 = 0.9 and C2 = 0.85. Ben scores C1 = 0.8 and C2 = 0.1. Both must receive distinct cameras.

Which assignment maximizes the total?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Ava C2 and Ben C1 totals 1.65. Ava C1 and Ben C2 totals 1.0. Distinctness leaves no other complete pair.

A camera is unavailable

Only C2 is available. Ava and Ben must each receive a camera and sharing is forbidden.

Which complete assignment should the desk display?

Reveal the result and reasoning

There is no feasible complete assignment. Giving C2 to both violates the rule. Waiting would need to be explicitly allowed before the desk could offer a partial allocation.

A tie at the desk

Only C1 is available, waiting scores zero, and both borrowers score 0.7 for C1. Ava requested it at 10:03 and Ben at 10:01.

Who gets the camera if the desk uses earlier request time to break score ties?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The two best allocations each total 0.7. The separately stated tie-break awards C1 to Ben at 10:01. Without the tie-break both allocations are equally good under the score objective.
