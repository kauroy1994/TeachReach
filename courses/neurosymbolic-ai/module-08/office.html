How is this different from putting a rule into the training loss?

In Module 07 a penalty changed which model weight we learned. Here the suitability scores are fixed before the decision. The decoder filters which outputs it can return. An allocation that reuses C1 fails the filter even if its raw score is high.

Does the highest total make an allocation fair?

The total answers one question about the stated suitability scores. It contains no information about waiting time or prior access unless the desk explicitly includes it. Two allocations with the same total can treat particular borrowers differently. Decide what matters before choosing the objective and tie-break.

Would listing every pair still work for a large equipment center?

For two people and two cameras there are only four pairs to inspect. For ten people with ten options each, listing every combination would examine ten billion possibilities before filtering. Larger problems can use assignment or constraint-solving algorithms that avoid testing every combination.
