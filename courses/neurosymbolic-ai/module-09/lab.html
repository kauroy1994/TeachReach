Explore a case

Same words, different item

Q = “camera C1 pickup”. P1 = “C1 pickup west”. P2 = “camera C2 pickup east”. Ignore case and count shared words.

Which passage wins on this score? Which mentions the intended item?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The score ties at two. P1 shares C1 and pickup. P2 shares camera and pickup. Only P1 mentions C1.

One changed edge

R4 says Ava reserves C2. D8 says C1 pickup_at West Desk. D9 says C2 pickup_at East Desk.

Where does the path end, and which records did you use?

Reveal the result and reasoning

It ends at East Desk. R4 leads from Ava to C2 and D9 leads from C2 to East Desk. D8 describes C1.

A missing relation

R4 says Ava reserves C1. S2 says C1 stored_at Vault. There is no pickup_at record for C1.

What can the desk tell Ava?

Reveal the result and reasoning

It can confirm the C1 reservation from R4. The pickup location is unknown. S2 would answer a question about storage.
