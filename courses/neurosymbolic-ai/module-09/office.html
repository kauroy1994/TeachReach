Why retrieve records when the desk already has a graph?

A graph helps when the fact you want has a named relation, such as pickup_at. Some instructions may exist only in a note. Retrieving the note lets a student read its wording and decide whether it applies.

Does an empty graph result mean the item has no pickup desk?

An empty result means the records you searched do not give a complete path. The campus could still have a pickup desk whose record is missing. Report what you searched and which link you still need.
