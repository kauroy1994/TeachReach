Explore a case

A supported location

For Monday, D8 assigns C1 to West Desk and D9 assigns C2 to East Desk. The draft says “C1 pickup is at West Desk on Monday.”

Which record supports this claim?

Reveal the result and reasoning

D8 matches the camera, location and day. D9 is about another camera.

A contradicted location

For Monday each camera has one desk. D8 assigns C1 to West Desk. The draft says “C1 pickup is at East Desk on Monday.”

Which verdict follows, and why?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Contradicted. With one desk for that camera and day, the current West Desk assignment excludes East Desk.

A plausible deadline

D8 assigns C1 to West Desk on Monday. H3 gives C2 a noon deadline. The draft says “Pick up C1 at West Desk by noon on Monday.”

Which part can you publish?

Reveal the result and reasoning

West Desk for C1 on Monday is supported by D8. The noon deadline for C1 is unknown. H3 concerns C2.
