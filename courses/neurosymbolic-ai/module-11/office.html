How does planning differ from the inference rules I used earlier?

An inference rule lets you conclude something about the facts you already have. Planning asks which actions could change the world so a desired fact becomes true. Here, a rule can say whether issue may run. Performing the modeled issue action changes reserved to issued.

Why keep a symbolic checker when a language model can suggest a sensible order?

A model can propose an order from a written request. A checker can follow the declared action rules and name the first missing requirement. If the rules omit an important desk requirement, the checker cannot catch that omission. Its conclusion depends on the model of the desk.
