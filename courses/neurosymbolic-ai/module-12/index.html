End-of-module exercises

Try each question before opening the hint or worked answer. The in-class variant asks you to apply the same skills to a new case. Assessment details →

Question 1

Lia’s active R8 is for C2, and T9 confirms camera orientation. Draft an answer to “Can I collect C1?” Assume the reservation records you retrieved may be incomplete.

Hint

An active reservation for one item does not settle the status of another item.

Reveal the worked answer

T9 confirms Lia’s camera orientation. R8 covers C2. Eligibility for C1 under P1 remains unknown until the desk checks for a current C1 reservation in Lia’s name.

Question 2

A draft says “Eligible under P1 because the reservation is active [R4] and orientation is complete [T7]. Pickup closes at 5.” The cited reservation and training records match. Edit the answer.

Hint

Check how many independent claims are present and which records cover each.

Reveal the worked answer

Keep the eligibility sentence with P1, R4 and T7. Omit the closing-time claim or retrieve a current hours record for the correct desk and day before including it.

Question 3

Create a two-case test for a desk system that always answers “no” when it cannot find an orientation record. Keep the other requirement satisfied.

Hint

Use a missing record in one case and an explicit negative record in the other.

Reveal the worked answer

Give both cases an active reservation. In case one, omit orientation evidence and expect “unknown; check orientation.” In case two, supply a current orientation-incomplete record and expect “cannot issue under P1.” A system that answers no twice fails the first case.
