Explore a case

A complete record

P1 requires an active reservation and completed camera orientation. R4 confirms Ava’s active C1 reservation. T7 confirms her camera orientation.

Write one sentence Ava could use to understand the decision.

Reveal the result and reasoning

Ava is eligible to collect C1 under P1 because her C1 reservation is active [R4] and she completed camera orientation [T7].

An explicit failed requirement

P1 applies. R4 confirms an active C1 reservation. T8 explicitly says Ava’s camera orientation is incomplete today.

Give the verdict and the explanation.

Reveal the result and reasoning

C1 cannot be issued under P1 while orientation is incomplete [T8], even though the reservation is active [R4]. Unlike a missing record, T8 establishes which requirement failed.

Two incompatible records

R4 confirms an active C1 reservation. T7 says complete and T8 says incomplete for the same student, equipment and time. Both have equal authority in this exercise.

Can the desk decide under P1? What can it tell Ava?

Reveal the result and reasoning

R4 settles the reservation. T7 and T8 disagree on orientation, which P1 requires. Withhold the eligibility verdict and ask the desk to reconcile those two records.
