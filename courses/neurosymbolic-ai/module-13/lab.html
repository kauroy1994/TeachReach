Explore a case

Two visits

Ava, camera C1. Request R7 has a check at minute 3. R8 has a handoff at minute 5. These are the only records.

Does either request have evidence of a check before its own handoff?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Neither does. R7 has no handoff record. R8 has no check record. Combining the visits would manufacture a completed sequence.

Whole-minute clock

R9 contains a check marked 10 and a handoff marked 10. The timestamps record whole minutes. No sequence numbers are available.

Which status should the desk show, and why?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Order unknown. The two entries establish that both actions were recorded during minute 10. They leave their order unresolved.

Late entry

R10 check occurred at minute 11 and was entered at 18. Handoff occurred at 15 and was entered at 16.

Which clock answers whether the physical steps followed the rule?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Use event times for the physical order. The recorded check at 11 preceded handoff at 15. Entry times 18 and 16 would reverse that order.
