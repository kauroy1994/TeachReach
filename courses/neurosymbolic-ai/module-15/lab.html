Identify a faulty record

R13 has score 0.94 for a real match. The program reads unavailable, while an independent inventory snapshot for that time confirms available.

Which component should you inspect first?

Reveal the result and reasoning

The photo decision agrees with the independent check. The availability input disagrees. Inspect the inventory record, its timestamp and how R13 was linked to it.

Count two denominators

A new test set has 4 eligible requests. A system sends 3, of which 2 are eligible.

Calculate precision and recall.

Reveal the result and reasoning

Precision is 2 of 3 sent requests. Recall is 2 of 4 eligible requests. One unnecessary review reduces precision and two missed eligible requests reduce recall.
