Can an explanation be faithful even when the decision is wrong?

Yes. A trace that says “I3 showed unavailable” can faithfully describe the program’s decision. If I3 was stale, that same trace helps a clerk find the problem. To judge correctness, compare the inputs and decision with independent evidence.

Should I always choose the method with the highest accuracy?

Ask what happens to each type of request. A desk that holds everything scores well when most requests should be held, but misses every eligible borrower. Count missed requests and unnecessary reviews, and relate both to the desk’s purpose.
