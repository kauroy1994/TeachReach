Explore a case

Event time reversed

T25 has note K20, score 0.89, event minute 17, and handoff scan H20 at minute 14. Both records identify T25.

Which finding and which evidence should the desk display?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Reversed. Candidate note K20 reports a check at 17, after the handoff in H20 at 14. A clerk can inspect those records before drawing conclusions about the actual handoff.

Check without a scan

T26 has note K21, score 0.98, event minute 12, and no handoff scan.

How should this differ from a documented reversal?

Reveal the result and reasoning

Needs review. K21 is a candidate check record, but the missing handoff time prevents ordering the events. Show K21 and the missing scan.

A misleading high score

T27 has note K22, “Label still needs checking,” score 0.91 at minute 9. Scan H22 records handoff at 12. Both IDs match.

What does the program print, and what should a reviewer do?

Reveal the result and reasoning

It labels T27 in order because the score passes and 9 is earlier than 12. The note itself describes unfinished work. Flag the note classification, record the correction and send the ticket for review.
