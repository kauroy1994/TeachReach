Where is the neurosymbolic part of this capstone?

The learned classifier proposes what free-text notes mean. The symbolic part checks explicit conditions about ticket identity, admissible evidence and time order. An error can arise in either part. Keeping the note and scan IDs lets a reviewer test the particular decision.

How does this relate to healthcare decision support?

Both an administrative audit and a clinical compliance study can ask what a record supports under an explicit rule. This projector exercise lets you practice representing evidence and uncertainty. Interpreting patient-care protocols requires clinical expertise, validated definitions and evaluation on appropriate data.

What should I hand in for the capstone?

Submit runnable code, a trace for each supplied ticket, the predicted and observed outcomes for your two added tests, and a short error analysis of the four independently reviewed tickets in the final exercise. A reader should be able to reproduce each label and locate its supporting records.
