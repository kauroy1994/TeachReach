Connect the reading to your model

Two desks each call a different camera C1. Adapt the campus graph so both desks can share their records. Then suppose the graph declares Borrower as the domain of requests and records that Ava requests the North Desk camera. Which new fact follows under RDF Schema reasoning? Can the desk now hand over the camera?

Reveal a worked answer

Give each camera its own global identifier and keep C1 as its local display label. Use the North Desk identifier as the object of Ava’s requests triple. Since requests has domain Borrower, a reasoner can infer that Ava is a Borrower. A desk rule would still need evidence of availability and any required orientation. Inferring a type from the schema supplies neither of those facts.
