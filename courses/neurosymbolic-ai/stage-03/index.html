Reading and extension

Retrieval-Augmented Generation for Knowledge-Intensive NLP Tasks

Read sections 2.1 and 2.2, focusing on RAG-Sequence. The retriever scores the similarity of learned numerical representations of the question and passages. For each retrieved passage, the generator assigns a probability to the complete answer. The model combines those probabilities across candidate passages, an operation called marginalization.

Patrick Lewis and colleagues (2020) · Open the reading ↗

Connect the reading to your model

In a two-passage example, the retriever gives weights 0.75 and 0.25. The generator assigns the complete answer “C1 is available” probabilities 0.20 and 0.80 when given those passages separately. Calculate the RAG-Sequence mixture. What extra check from the desk exercise would you perform before showing that answer to Ava?

Reveal a worked answer

The weighted probability is 0.75 × 0.20 + 0.25 × 0.80 = 0.35. It measures the model’s probability of generating those words. In the desk exercise you can look up C1 by its exact identifier and inspect the current availability record. A passage about a different camera, or an old record, cannot justify a claim about C1 now. Keep the supporting record ID or report that the evidence is missing.
