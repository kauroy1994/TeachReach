Connect the reading to your model

Transfer that division of labor to the projector capstone. Suppose two names for the same projector are assigned to different item IDs. Which downstream finding could change although the event times never changed? Design a test that distinguishes a naming error from a rule error. What could you conclude if high-scoring tickets also had shorter queues?

Reveal a worked answer

A valid handoff scan might be filed under the wrong item, leaving the matching ticket without its scan. First compare the mapped IDs against an independent clerk’s labels. Then give the time rule the corrected IDs and replay it with unchanged timestamps. If the result recovers, the mapping contributed to the error. Report the queue pattern as an association. Busy periods could affect both record completeness and queues, so that comparison alone cannot establish that improving documentation shortens waits.
