CS 696 · Stage 01 of 04

Represent rules and their sources

Trace a reuse decision through two versions of a regulatory filing. Build a representation that lets a reviewer inspect the decision and its source.

Start with your experience writing and testing small programs.

Module 01

Frame a document-reuse question

Define a unit of reuse, locate a source value and its governing passages, and state a claim about moving that value that could fail an audit.

Bring the four-field source inventory, one six-part decision unit, two passage pairs and the six audit rows from the exercise.

Module 02

Decide which moves the evidence allows

Represent a cross-edition correspondence, distinguish a field match from an authorized action and leave an actionable review request when a needed fact is missing.

Bring the five candidate edges, the two direct-copy decisions from Module 01 and the role-and-note ledger before and after the extra evidence arrives.

Reading for the meeting

W3C · PROV Model Primer

Identify an entity, an activity and a derivation in your document mapping. Which information would a second reviewer need to recover the original clause?

Before you move on

Pass the ledger columns source_component, target_field, clause_ids, finding and next_action into Module 03. A new workflow document will pose a related question. Given stated conditions and a changed input, which step may execute, which evidence permits it, and where must execution pause?
